# Online Crime Management System

**Type:** Minor project · Third year · 2022  
**Technology:** Java  
**Team:** Collaborative four-member college team

## Overview
An information-management system focused on storing and organising criminal records, case details, complaint records and case histories.

## Scope
- Maintain criminal record information.
- Organise complaint and case details.
- Keep case history information accessible.
